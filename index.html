Know the facts

5 facts every woman
should know.

  1. Early detection saves lives.Mortality drops when breast cancer is found and treated early.
  2. Small lumps are easier to treat.Tumors that haven't reached the lymph nodes are more likely to be treated successfully.
  3. Screening starts at 50.Mammography programs usually target women aged 50 to 69. Ask your doctor what fits your history.
  4. Men can get it too.About 0.5% to 1% of breast cancers occur in men.
  5. Progress is real.In high-income countries, breast cancer mortality fell 40% between the 1980s and 2020.

Source: World Health Organization.

Rise in Pink is Unicoin Foundation's campaign for Breast Cancer Awareness Month. It raises funds for screening, treatment access and support for women with breast cancer, and rewards donors with unicoins.

The campaign is led by Unicoin Foundation in partnership with [Beneficiary foundation], which receives the donations.
